The day at a glance
No climate deal, a widening Iran conflict, and a drone war reaching the Baltic
Climate · Antalya

COP31 ends with no deal

After two weeks and two days of overtime, the 31st climate summit failed to adopt the Antalya Pact. The EU and others called the final draft too weak on fossil fuels; chair Murat Kurum said there was “nothing to do”, and left the stage as delegates spoke.

196 countries, no agreement: the Antalya Pact was not adopted.
Middle East · Bahrain

Iran says it struck two US bases

Revolutionary Guard missiles and drones hit US positions at Al-Riffa and Awali, which Tehran called retaliation for airspace violations. A tanker struck a mine and burned off Hormuz.

Ukraine · Baltic

Four killed near Kyiv; a Russian ship burns

Drone strikes hit flats outside the capital. In the Gulf of Finland a Russian cargo ship was set ablaze by drones and 16 crew were rescued by Finland.

Yemen · Sanaa

Air strikes hit the capital’s fuel depots

Four raids on Sanaa destroyed the city’s largest fuel station and damaged the state oil company; six were hurt. The Houthis blamed the Saudi-led coalition.

Americas · United States

Masked agents seize two at Seattle airport

Immigration officers took two people off a jet bridge as passengers boarded, delaying the flight. In California a billionaire-tax drive lost a court round.
