Climate · World

COP31 ends without adopting a pact

The 31st climate summit closed two days into overtime with no agreement: the EU and others rejected a third draft as too weak on fossil fuels, and chair Murat Kurum saw no consensus and put no new text forward. (Reuters, Xinhua, UN)

2MANAMA SAT CLAIM · SUN ANSWER Target disputed
Middle East · Bahrain

Manama: the "radar strike" hit a factory

Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed missiles and drones hit radar sites at Al-Riffa and Awali on Saturday night. On Sunday Bahrain's interior ministry called the target a civilian industrial site, said two people were hurt and denied a base was struck. (IRNA, BNA, Xinhua)

3KYIV 4 KILLED Reported
Europe · Ukraine

Four killed near Kyiv as drones hit flats

Russian drone strikes hit apartment blocks outside the capital, killing four, as attacks widened. In the Baltic the day before, drones set a Russian cargo ship ablaze; Finland rescued its 16 crew. (NHK, Reuters)
